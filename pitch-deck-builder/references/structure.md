# The 6-step pitching structure

| Part | Share of slides and minutes | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | 30% | 1 Problem and pain point · 2 Impact |
| Solution | 40% | 3 The solution idea · 4 Benefits and value · 5 How it works |
| Summary | 30% | 6 Summary and goal |

Example timing: a 20-minute pitch is about 6 / 8 / 6 minutes. A 10-minute pitch is about
3 / 4 / 3 minutes and 6-8 slides.

## The steps
1. **Problem and pain point.** Use the audience's words. Open with a thought-provoking
   question. End with 2-3 concrete pain questions; these are the thread.
2. **Impact.** What each pain costs if nothing changes. One big sourced figure, or one
   strong word, per pain.
3. **Solution idea.** Reveal the product as the answer, with one sentence that explains
   the name.
4. **Benefits and value.** Outcomes, one per pain, plus a short before/after.
   Benefits come BEFORE features.
5. **How it works.** One feature per pain, each with an "ANSWERS: <question>" line.
   Stay at "what"; the full "how" belongs in the demo.
6. **Summary and goal.** A real section, not one slide. Default pattern (4 slides):
   1. **Recap** "Three questions. Three answers.": per pain, question -> cost -> answer.
   2. **Why us**: sourced proof (partner tier, customers, awards, certifications).
   3. **What happens next**: 3-4 small concrete steps and one reassurance line. Promise
      only what the team can really do.
   4. **CTA**: close the loop on the opening question, one button, a contact line.

For a short slot, merge Recap and Why us, and keep Next steps and CTA.

## Typical story arc (familiar -> new)
1. Opening question (no answer yet)
2. What they already use (familiar tools, real logos)
3. What is missing / what must connect
4. Name the concept with the single analogy
5. The 2-3 pain questions
6. Impact
7. Reveal the product
8. Benefits (before features)
9. One feature slide per pain
10. Recap, Why us, Next steps, CTA

## The thread rule
Each pain keeps the same icon and the same words from the question slide to the recap:

| Pain question | Impact | Benefit | Feature | Recap answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who builds them? | weeks of IT time | Faster | Builder | built in a day |

If a pain disappears halfway, either bring it back or remove it from the question slide.

## 5-sentence test
Write the deck as five sentences: the situation, the problem, the cost, the answer, the
next step. If you cannot, the story is not clear yet. Fix the outline before building.
